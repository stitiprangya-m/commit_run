# Viability Analysis – Subscription Management System

## 1. Overview

The Subscription Management System is a multi-tenant B2B SaaS application designed for organizations that sell products or services through recurring subscription plans.

The product aims to centralize subscription operations, customer management, recurring invoicing, payment tracking, and business monitoring within a single platform.

Its commercial viability depends on whether organizations find sufficient value in these capabilities to adopt the platform and pay recurring subscription fees.

The initial business strategy will focus on validating customer demand, establishing a reliable subscription-based revenue model, and building long-term customer relationships.

## 2. Target Market

The primary target market consists of small and growing organizations that manage recurring products or services.

Potential customer segments include:

- Subscription-based service providers.
- Coaching and educational businesses.
- Fitness and membership-based businesses.
- Recurring service providers.
- Product and service rental businesses.
- Small and medium-sized enterprises managing recurring customer billing.

The initial focus should be organizations that currently manage customer subscriptions, billing records, and payment tracking through spreadsheets or disconnected systems.

**Market assumption:** These businesses may benefit from a centralized subscription management platform. Actual demand must be validated through customer interviews and early adoption.

## 3. Customer Problem & Product Value

Organizations managing recurring subscriptions often need to coordinate multiple operational activities.

The platform addresses these needs through:

| Business Problem | Proposed Solution |
|---|---|
| Disorganized customer records | Centralized customer management |
| Manual subscription tracking | Subscription lifecycle management |
| Repetitive invoice creation | Automatic recurring invoice generation |
| Difficulty tracking payments | Manual payment recording and allocation |
| Unclear outstanding balances | Invoice balance and payment status tracking |
| Uncontrolled staff access | Role-based permissions |
| Limited business visibility | Organization dashboards |
| Scattered customer information | Customer portal |

The primary value proposition is providing organizations with a centralized system for managing their recurring subscription operations.

## 4. Business Model Viability

The recommended business model is **B2B SaaS (Business-to-Business Software as a Service)**.

Organizations will pay a recurring subscription fee to access the platform.

Potential billing options:

- Monthly organization subscriptions.
- Annual organization subscriptions.

The platform will initially focus on recurring SaaS subscription revenue rather than transaction-based commissions.

Payment gateway integration is excluded from the MVP. Therefore, the initial platform subscription collection process will be handled separately through manual payment verification.

The business model can be expanded through additional subscription tiers once customer demand and usage patterns are understood.

## 5. Competitive Positioning

The product will operate in the subscription management and recurring billing software market.

Its proposed positioning is based on combining multiple operational capabilities within one platform:

- Multi-tenant organization management.
- Customer and staff management.
- Configurable staff permissions.
- Product and subscription plan management.
- Subscription lifecycle tracking.
- Automated invoice generation.
- Manual payment allocation.
- Customer-facing subscription and invoice access.
- Organization-level dashboards.

The product should compete on workflow completeness, usability, affordability, and operational convenience rather than price alone.

A detailed competitor analysis and feature comparison will be required before finalizing market positioning and pricing.

## 6. Customer Acquisition Strategy

Initial customer acquisition should focus on direct and relatively low-cost channels.

Recommended approaches:

1. Direct outreach to relevant businesses.
2. Product demonstrations for potential customers.
3. Referrals from existing customers.
4. Professional communities and business networks.
5. LinkedIn and other relevant social platforms.
6. Partnerships with business consultants and service providers.

During the initial stage, founder-led customer acquisition is recommended.

The primary objective should be acquiring a small group of paying organizations and understanding their actual usage, expectations, and feedback.

## 7. Customer Retention & Sustainability

Long-term business sustainability depends on retaining organizations and consistently delivering value.

The following factors will be important:

- Reliable recurring invoice generation.
- Accurate subscription and billing records.
- Convenient customer management.
- Reliable payment tracking.
- Useful organization dashboards.
- Secure staff access.
- Consistent application performance.
- Responsive customer support.

Since the platform becomes part of an organization's recurring operational workflow, reliability and data accuracy will be especially important for customer retention.

## 8. Revenue Sustainability

The business will primarily depend on recurring organization subscription fees.

Important financial metrics to monitor include:

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| MRR | Monthly Recurring Revenue |
| ARR | Annual Recurring Revenue |
| CAC | Customer Acquisition Cost |
| LTV | Customer Lifetime Value |
| Churn Rate | Percentage of customers cancelling subscriptions |
| Conversion Rate | Percentage of prospects becoming paying customers |
| ARPA | Average Revenue Per Organization |

For sustainable growth:

- Customer lifetime value should exceed customer acquisition cost.
- Customer acquisition expenses should remain controlled.
- Recurring revenue should cover operating expenses.
- Customer churn should be monitored and reduced.
- Pricing should reflect the actual value delivered.

Commercial viability cannot be established through projected subscription revenue alone. Actual customer acquisition, payment, and retention data will be required.

## 9. Risks & Business Challenges

| Risk | Potential Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Low market demand | Limited customer acquisition | Validate demand before scaling |
| Strong competition | Difficulty differentiating | Focus on workflow completeness and usability |
| Pricing mismatch | Low conversion | Test pricing with potential customers |
| High acquisition costs | Reduced profitability | Prioritize direct outreach and referrals |
| Customer churn | Unstable recurring revenue | Improve product reliability and customer value |
| Limited product adoption | Low engagement | Simplify onboarding and provide demonstrations |
| Operational dependency | Customer dissatisfaction | Maintain billing accuracy and application reliability |

## 10. Growth Opportunities

Once the MVP demonstrates consistent customer demand, the business can explore additional opportunities.

Potential future improvements include:

- Multiple subscription pricing tiers.
- Advanced reporting and analytics.
- Payment gateway integration.
- Advanced billing and discount management.
- Quotation management.
- Enterprise-level capabilities.
- Additional integrations.
- Premium onboarding and support services.

These opportunities should be prioritized according to customer feedback, business requirements, and revenue potential.

They are not part of the current MVP scope.

## 11. Market Validation Strategy

Before investing heavily in customer acquisition and additional development, the product should be validated with real businesses.

Recommended validation activities:

1. Identify potential businesses within the target market.
2. Understand their existing subscription and billing workflows.
3. Conduct product demonstrations.
4. Collect feedback on usability and required functionality.
5. Validate willingness to pay.
6. Acquire initial paying organizations.
7. Monitor product usage and subscription retention.
8. Improve the product based on actual customer feedback.

The initial objective should be to establish whether businesses are willing to adopt and pay for the platform.

## 13. Conclusion

The Subscription Management System presents a promising B2B SaaS business opportunity by addressing recurring operational requirements for organizations that sell subscription-based products and services.

Its centralized approach to customer management, subscription tracking, recurring invoicing, payment allocation, and business monitoring provides a clear proposed value proposition.

However, commercial success cannot be guaranteed through product development alone.

The business must validate actual market demand, willingness to pay, customer acquisition costs, subscription retention, and operating expenses through real-world adoption.

The recommended approach is to launch the focused MVP, acquire initial paying organizations, collect feedback, and use actual business performance to guide future product and pricing decisions.

**Final Assessment: Promising – Recommended to Proceed with Market Validation.**
